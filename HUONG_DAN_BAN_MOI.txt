BAN MOI: Seller minimum + Bidder flexible step

1. Seller nhap "Buoc gia toi thieu" o muc quan ly san pham.
2. Bidder dat gia THU CONG: nhap gia tuy y >= gia hien tai + muc toi thieu.
3. Bidder Auto-Bid: nhap Gia toi da + Buoc gia Auto-Bid (>= Seller minimum).
4. Neu gia tran sat den, buoc Auto-Bid cuoi co the nho hon buoc mong muon
   nhung khong duoc nho hon muc toi thieu cua Seller.
5. Chuyen file patch vao dung THU MUC GOC CO pom.xml; khong tao thu muc con trung ten.
6. Tat server/client cu, backup data/state.json, ghi de file, build lai:
      .\mvnw.cmd clean verify
      .\run-server.bat
      .\run-client.bat    (terminal moi)
7. Neu may con dung client/JAR cu, thoa tac se khong thay doi.
8. Ban patch nay ap dung tren Online-Auction-System-2026-Seller-Bid-Step-CORRECTED.
   Neu khong chac project hien tai o phien ban nao, dung ban FULL.zip roi
   copy data/state.json tu du an cu sang dung vi tri.
